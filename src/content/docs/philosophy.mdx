# The Philosophy of Curation

The internet has an abundance of fonts, but an acute shortage of discernment.

Open-source font directories have become dumping grounds for experimental student projects, incomplete character sets, and unoptimized files that drag down Core Web Vitals.

> "Perfection is achieved, not when there is nothing more to add, but when there is nothing left to take away."
> — Antoine de Saint-Exupéry

---

## The Four Golden Pillars

Every typeface hosted or indexed on NoBadFonts must satisfy four non-negotiable standards:

### 1. Optical Balance & Kerning Rigor
A font is only as good as its letter spacing. We reject fonts with erratic spacing around common pairings like `AV`, `To`, `Ta`, and `We`. A great typeface looks balanced straight out of the box without requiring manual CSS letter-spacing hacks.

### 2. Multi-Weight Versatility
A single regular weight font is insufficient for production software. We prioritize type families providing at least 3 distinct optical weights (e.g., Regular, Medium, Bold) with accompanying italic slants.

<Callout type="note" title="Production Rule">
Fonts with only a single ultra-light or display cut are tagged as decorative and excluded from general body recommendations.
</Callout>

### 3. Complete Character Sets
Missing accented characters (`é`, `ñ`, `ü`) or punctuation marks break internationalization. Fonts in NoBadFonts must cover standard Latin Extended glyph sets at minimum.

### 4. Zero Licensing Ambiguity
We strictly index fonts under clear open-source or permissive licenses (such as the SIL Open Font License). You should never have to fear receiving a legal cease-and-desist for using a webfont on a commercial client project.

## Curation vs. Algorithmic Popularity

Most directories rank fonts based on raw download counts. This creates a feedback loop where the top 10 fonts get 95% of traffic, while incredible masterpieces by independent type designers remain buried on page 42.

NoBadFonts evaluates fonts on **design merit**, **typographic voice**, and **web performance** — shining a light on hidden gems that elevate your visual identity.
