# NoBadFonts Model Context Protocol (MCP)

The **NoBadFonts MCP Server** exposes the entire curated font database, typography taxonomy, and pairing engine to AI assistants through the open [Model Context Protocol](https://modelcontextprotocol.io).

Instead of an AI hallucinating non-existent Google Font names or proposing broken CSS pairings, connecting NoBadFonts empowers your AI agent to recommend verified, high-performance typography in real time.

---

## Why Connect NoBadFonts to your AI?

* **Contextual Typography Recommendations:** Ask your AI *"Recommend an editorial serif pairing for a luxury fintech landing page"*, and it queries live metadata.
* **Accurate Open-Source Licensing:** Your AI validates commercial license rights before suggesting or downloading assets.
* **Instant Code Generation:** The AI can generate ready-to-paste Tailwind CSS tokens and `@font-face` definitions verified against our glyph subsets.
* **Automated Research & Metadata:** AI tools can research foundry links, identify license types, and propose catalog improvements.
