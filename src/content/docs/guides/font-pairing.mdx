# The Art & Science of Font Pairing

Pairing typefaces is one of the highest-leverage design decisions in frontend development. The right combination establishes visual hierarchy, creates emotional resonance, and makes long-form text effortless to read.

---

## The Core Rule: Contrast, Not Conflict

When combining two fonts, your goal is **intentional contrast**. If two typefaces are too similar (for example, pairing two slightly different geometric sans-serifs), the interface looks like an accidental CSS bug.

<Callout type="warning" title="Avoid Conflict">
Never pair two distinct geometric sans fonts together (e.g. Montserrat with Inter). Either choose fonts with distinct optical classifications, or use a single multi-weight type family.
</Callout>

---

## 3 Proven Pairing Formulas

### 1. The Editorial Classic (Display Serif + Clean Sans)
* **Heading:** Characterful Serif with high stroke contrast.
* **Body:** Neutral, highly legible Grotesk or Neo-Grotesk Sans.
* **Mood:** Thoughtful, authoritative, literary, luxurious.

### 2. The Modern Tech (Clean Sans + Geometric Accents)
* **Heading:** Expressive Sans or Bold Neo-Grotesk.
* **Body:** Neutral Sans with large x-height.
* **Accent/Code:** Monospace with clear punctuation distinctions.
* **Mood:** Fast, efficient, contemporary, professional.

### 3. The Digital Artisan (Warm Humanist Sans + Warm Serif)
* **Heading:** Humanist Sans (e.g. Outfit, Satoshi).
* **Body:** Modern Transitional Serif (e.g. Source Serif, Merriweather).
* **Mood:** Approachable, creative, human, editorial.

## Optical Hierarchy Checklist

1. **Scale Jump:** Ensure your `h1` is at least **2.25x to 3x** the font size of your body text.
2. **Weight Contrast:** If your heading is extra bold (`700` or `800`), keep your body text at regular (`400`).
3. **x-Height Compatibility:** Ensure the lowercase letter heights (x-height) of both fonts visually harmonize when placed in proximity.
4. **Letter-Spacing:** Tighten tracking slightly on large headings (`-0.02em`), and loosen tracking on small uppercase labels (`+0.05em`).
